About LittleBlossom

Learn about our story, mission, and the values that guide us.

Our History

Precious Tebogo Mokgobu founded LittleBlossom Inclusive Daycare in 2026 with a simple but powerful vision: to create a daycare where every child belongs.

The idea came about when the founder recognized a critical gap in the Gauteng childcare market — very few daycares openly welcome and support children with disabilities, different personalities, diverse cultural or religious backgrounds, or from varying family and economic circumstances.

To develop this daycare, extensive research was conducted on inclusive early childhood education. This included studying best practices for inclusive education, learning about different personality types and how to support them, researching various cultural and religious needs, and consulting with child development specialists and occupational therapists.

LittleBlossom Inclusive Daycare officially opened its doors in February 2026 at 56 Blossom Street, Sandton, Johannesburg.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing, and inclusive daycare environment where every child — regardless of ability, personality, background, religion, or family status — is celebrated, supported, and given the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Our Vision

To become Gauteng's leading inclusive daycare center, setting the standard for how children of all abilities and backgrounds learn and grow together, and to inspire other daycare centers to embrace inclusivity as a core value.

Our Core Values

Inclusion

Every child is welcome, regardless of ability, personality, background, or religion. Diversity is celebrated.

Acceptance

We teach children to embrace differences in themselves and others, fostering a culture of kindness and respect.

Individualized Care

Every child receives care and support tailored to their unique needs, interests, and personality.

Safety

A physically and emotionally safe environment is a non-negotiable right for every child in our care.

Love

Every child is treated with genuine warmth, kindness, and respect, creating a sense of belonging.

Growth

We help every child develop cognitively, socially, emotionally, and physically, celebrating their unique milestones.
