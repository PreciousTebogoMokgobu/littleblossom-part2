Welcome to LittleBlossom

We are a warm and inclusive daycare center in Gauteng that welcomes every child exactly as they are. Whether your child has special needs, a unique personality, or comes from any background, they will find love, support, and a place to grow at LittleBlossom.

Why Choose Us?

Truly Inclusive

We welcome children of ALL abilities, personalities, cultures, and backgrounds. No child is ever turned away.

Individualized Care

Every child receives a personalized care plan tailored to their unique needs, interests, and personality.

Qualified Staff

Our team includes trained ECD practitioners and special needs educators who truly care about your child.

Affordable Fees

We offer competitive fees with financial assistance available to ensure every family can access quality care.
