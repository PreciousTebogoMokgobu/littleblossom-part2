Our Services

Discover the inclusive care and programs we offer for your child.

What We Offer

At LittleBlossom, we provide a range of services designed to meet the unique needs of every child. Our programs are designed to be inclusive, nurturing, and developmentally appropriate.

Infant Care

Nurturing care for babies aged 0-18 months. Focus on attachment, sensory development, and individual routines.

Key Features:

  • Individual feeding schedules
  • Sensory play activities
  • Safe sleep environment
  • Daily developmental tracking

Toddler Program

For children aged 18 months to 3 years. Focus on exploration, language development, and social skills.

Key Features:

  • Language and vocabulary building
  • Creative arts and crafts
  • Social interaction activities
  • Basic routine and structure

Preschool Program

For children aged 3-5 years. Focus on school readiness, cognitive development, and creativity.

Key Features:

  • Early literacy and numeracy
  • Science and discovery
  • Creative expression
  • School readiness preparation

Inclusive Support

Individualized support for children with special needs, disabilities, or unique learning requirements.

Key Features:

  • Individualized Education Plans
  • Occupational therapy support
  • Speech therapy integration
  • Sensory-friendly environment

Nutrition Program

Healthy, balanced meals prepared daily. We accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies.

Key Features:

  • Fresh, locally sourced ingredients
  • Allergy-friendly options
  • Cultural dietary considerations
  • Nutrition education for children

Outdoor Play

Safe, supervised outdoor play areas for physical development and exploration.

Key Features:

  • Secure, fenced play areas
  • Age-appropriate equipment
  • Gardening activities
  • Nature exploration
